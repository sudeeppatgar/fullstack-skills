## Rules

- A repository-level license may not cover every file in a repository.
- Inspect the specific skill's license and bundled assets.
- Preserve copyright notices and license texts as required.
- Do not relicense third-party content as MIT just because this repository uses MIT for original work.
- When permission is unclear, do not copy the skill; link to upstream or ask the rights holder.
